How it works

  
    
    
  • Drift by rank bucket. Each game's rank is a random walk. The drift
      (rank change per 6h) is estimated from every pair of consecutive reads,
      separately for rank buckets 1–3, 4–10, 11–20, 21–40, 41–200 — top games
      cool off, mid-chart games climb.
    • 
    
  • Own velocity. The drift is blended 70/30 with the game's own rank
      velocity over the last 40 hours (clamped to ±15 ranks per 6h).
    • 
    
  • Diffusion. Uncertainty widens with the horizon
      (σ² = 2.0² + 0.7·hours). Per-place probabilities are the time-average of
      each game's Gaussian mass on that place over the 72h window.
    • 
    
  • Unnamed mass. 30% of every place is left unnamed: about a sixth of
      top-10 slots in the archive go to games never seen before.
    • 
    
  • Eligibility taper. Games whose 28-day Up-and-Coming eligibility ends
      inside the window are tapered by the fraction they survive.
    •

  Python original — same inputs give the same top 10 and probabilities within
  1.1e-6 (run node verify.cjs in demo/). No server,
  no tracking, no data leaves your browser.
